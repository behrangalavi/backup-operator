# 0083. Row-counter scan errors fall back to stats, never hard-fail the dump

**Status:** Accepted.

`RowCounter.scan()` captured `scanner.Err()` into a field but `Close()` always returned `nil`, so a parse error was silently swallowed. The dangerous consequence was specific to the empty-dump detector: a single dump line exceeding the scanner's 10 MB buffer (`bufio.ErrTooLong` — e.g. a wide row with a large blob under extended-insert) aborts the scan mid-stream, leaving the per-table counts truncated. Those truncated counts then flow into `LooksEmpty`, which would hard-fail an otherwise healthy large-row backup as `dump-empty-content`. Fix: `Close()` now returns `scanErr` (plus an `Err()` accessor for reads after Close — safe without a lock because the `done`-channel close establishes happens-before), and the pipeline checks it: on a non-nil scan error it logs and leaves `dumpCounts` nil so `BuildVerification` falls back to pre/post stats comparison instead of acting on the partial count. Trade-off accepted: in the rare case where the dump is *genuinely* empty AND the scan errored, we miss the count-based check — but the stats comparison still backstops it, and a false-negative on a rare edge beats false-failing every large-row dump. The `Active()`/`Err()` split keeps the three states distinct: inactive (mongo/redis, no parser), active+clean (trust counts), active+errored (distrust counts, fall back).
